Fishion Rebrand

Brand Identity & Visual Direction

Since 1989, Fishion Therapy Center has been located in the heart of Chinatown,
on the 2nd floor of the On Leong Chinese Merchants Association building.
In this rebranding, I want to incorporate the buildings stunning visuals to make Fishion a brand that can be instantly recognized for the long term and first time customers.
